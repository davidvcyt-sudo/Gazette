# The Wordette — banned words

# These words are not in the game at all. They are never the word of the day,
# and typing one is refused the same way a made-up word is: "Not in the word
# list". Nothing about them is shown to the reader.
#
# This is the stronger of the two lists. Its neighbour, blocklist.txt, only
# stops a word being the ANSWER while leaving it typeable — that one is for
# ordinary words like "slave" or "henry" that simply do not make good puzzles.

# naked, moron, leper and about two dozen more — was deliberately left in the
# game, because refusing a normal word confuses a player.

# line. Add or remove entries and run `npm run db:reset`. Lines starting with
# # are comments.
